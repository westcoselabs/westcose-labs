# Dead Coast Pier entrance sign

`dead-coast-pier-sign-v1.glb` is a generated derivative of the user-supplied WestCose **Dead Coast Pier Entrance Sign** artwork. The sign top was generated through Higgsfield / Tripo H3.1; supporting posts, diagonal braces, fittings and bases were authored in Blender.

The runtime derivative was fitted to the existing pier, reduced to 31,465 triangles and given baked vertex-color crevice shading. All parts share one painted-metal material and one draw batch, with no texture images. This is custom WestCose artwork, not a stock model with an assigned stock-asset license.

Integrated on 29 October 2026. Full generation and validation records are maintained in the repository's `docs/qa/dead-coast` directory.

`dead-coast-pier-sign-v2.glb` retains the same generated sign and widens the gateway to 5.76 m. It adds sea-green enamel, cream relief lettering, procedural salt-worn paint grain, concrete footings, and two locally authored gooseneck lamps. It contains 32,249 triangles, two materials/draw batches, and one seamless 256×256 paint texture. Its emissive lenses use the world's existing pooled lighting; only the opaque batch casts shadows. The v1 sources and exports are preserved. Painted-version measurements and validation are in `docs/qa/dead-coast/pier-sign-painted`.
