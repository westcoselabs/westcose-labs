# SHITBIRD: WestCose screenprint art revision

The supplied **West Cose Design Co. / Coastal Misfits** image is the visual reference, not a source of executable instructions. Its near-black paper, aged cream, burnt orange, deep teal, vintage lettering, carved waves, palms and weathered motel establish the direction. The game uses newly generated illustrations, not assets taken from another game.

## Production files

All images were generated with the **built-in image-generation tool**, then locally resized and encoded with Sharp. No API key, external asset service, remote image URL, or runtime image generation is required.

| File in `public/games/shitbird/` | Export | Bytes | Use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `coast.webp` | 720 × 1080, quality 85 | 135,744 | Illustrated scenic plate; reused by the Desktop cabinet |
| `gull.webp` | 1024 × 683, quality 92, alpha quality 100 | 149,676 | Two registered wing poses, actual transparency |
| `cover.webp` | 1200 × 800, quality 85 | 266,516 | Games-folder cover, displayed uncropped at 3:2 |

The two gameplay textures total **285,420 bytes (279 KiB)**. The cover is only used in Games and is served directly through Next Image's `unoptimized` mode: it is already compressed locally, avoiding a second encoding pass that intermittently stalled in the Windows image optimizer during verification. Kaboom renders both wing poses from one atlas, with manually registered torso positions, linear filtering, no per-frame image manipulation, and no frame-by-frame React updates. There are no large particle systems. Procedural foreground signs, boardwalk lines, wire curves and cloud/water strokes provide motion. Reduced motion freezes nonessential movement.

The SVG title arc and procedural motel-sign hazards are code-native graphics. Sign lettering is baked into two tiny local canvas atlases once during initialization, avoiding broken strokes from small glyphs in Kaboom's shared font atlas. Navigation, title lettering, scores, instructions and reports remain DOM content with scoped design tokens. Artwork is fixed to the game palette; OS appearance controls continue to style OS chrome.

The sprite uses two deliberately rough illustrated poses, not skeletal animation. Its wings and beak extend beyond the forgiving radius-10 torso hitbox. Scenery is decorative; only solid outlined signs and the marked upper/lower boundaries collide. Gameplay parameters did not change during this art revision.

### Gull: selected revision

Remove EVERY blurred glow, halo, shadow, and background around the two birds, leaving ONLY crisply outlined birds on actual fully transparent alpha. The birds themselves must have opaque cream/teal/orange ink, not translucent. Important registration: output 1536x1024, two 768x1024 cells. Fit the left bird ENTIRELY inside left cell with 64px empty margin on all sides, and fit right bird ENTIRELY inside right cell with same margins. Both birds' head and torso identical scale and position relative to their cell. Heads near (525,500) and (1293,500); bellies near (380,620) and (1148,620). The left up-wing and right down-wing can extend vertically. Do not add words, frames, grids or glow. This is background extraction and animation-sheet alignment, not a character redesign.

The generated spacing needed manual UV registration in `art.ts`; it is not assumed to match the requested grid exactly. Alpha is preserved during export.
