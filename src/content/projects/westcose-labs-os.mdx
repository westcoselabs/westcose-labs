## The brief

WestCose Labs OS turns a conventional portfolio into a fictional workstation and
matching pocket device. The design goal is memorable presentation with modern,
predictable browser behavior underneath.

## The system decision

The URL selects the content. A typed shell resolver selects Desktop OS, Pocket
OS, or Normal View around that same route. This keeps direct links, refresh,
Back and Forward, metadata, and server-rendered content intact.

## Constraints

- Keep route content out of duplicated shell-specific files.
- Keep startup, lock, and Pocket Home inside the usable mobile viewport.
- Keep game engines, large media, and unverified integrations out of the first load.
- Preserve keyboard, reduced-motion, high-contrast, and visible alternatives.

## What is implemented

The repository contains typed app, route, project, placement, and notification
registries; versioned local preferences and Pocket session state; draggable and
resizable desktop windows; a two-page Pocket Home; and a conventional Normal
View that renders without completing the operating-system interactions.

## Verification

The implementation is covered by unit, component, Playwright, visual, and
automated accessibility tests. Public links and final production screenshots are
shown only after owner verification.
